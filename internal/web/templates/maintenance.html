Checks and housekeeping

Runs the full PRAGMA integrity_check and reports the verdict.

Runs PRAGMA quick_check: much faster than the full check, suitable for routine use.

Runs PRAGMA foreign_key_check and reports orphaned rows, if any.

Rebuilds the file and returns free pages to disk. Can take a while on large caches.

Runs PRAGMA optimize and checkpoints the WAL. Cheap and safe while the service runs.

Writes pending WAL frames into the main file and truncates the -wal file.

Runs ANALYZE to rebuild query-planner statistics after large changes.

Runs REINDEX. Useful after a large deletion; can take a while.

Does not re-enable a source; it only resets the error state and backoff.

Danger zone
